# Polo Tecnológico del Paraná - Branding Research

## Organization Overview

**Name:** Polo Tecnológico del Paraná  
**Full Name:** Polo Tecnológico del Paraná  
**Location:** Paraná, Entre Ríos, Argentina  
**Type:** Non-profit civil association  
**Tagline:** "Impulsamos la economía del conocimiento en la región" (We drive the knowledge economy in the region)  
**Alternative Tagline:** "Conectando a la Región con la demanda mundial de negocios" (Connecting the Region with global business demand)

## Mission & Vision

The Polo Tecnológico del Paraná connects companies, universities, entrepreneurs, and institutions to create community and generate transformative challenges for the local economy and the export of knowledge worldwide. They work on:

- Technology transfer
- Strengthening and creating new companies (primarily within the Knowledge Economy Law)
- Generating new jobs
- Providing high-level services and products

## Key Statistics

- **Primary Sector:** Software and digital services
- **Direct Employment:** 700+ people
- **Estimated Regional Impact:** 2,000+ people
- **Anniversary:** 10 years of operation

## Recent Development

**MiradorTec Lab** - A new technological center launched in Paraná designed to be the epicenter of the entrepreneurial ecosystem of Entre Ríos. Features:

- Mentoring services
- Coworking spaces
- Investor connections
- Specialized training programs
- Direct links with the local academic sector
