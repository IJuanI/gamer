# GamER — Brand System

**Source**: GamER Branding RAW.pdf
**Last reviewed**: 2026-04-11

## 1. Identity

- **Brand name**: `GamER` (stylized)
- **Full name**: Entre Ríos Gamers
- **Region**: Entre Ríos, Argentina
- **Category**: Gaming community / esports organization

## 2. Logo

### Structure
- Primary wordmark: `GAMER`
- `GAM` = purple (`#B339C4`) · `ER` = green (`#84C552`)
- Tagline: `ENTRE RÍOS GAMERS` in neutral gray, centered below wordmark

### Approved variants
- Full color on light background
- One-color dark version
- Reverse version for purple background
- Black and white / monochrome

### Rules
- Do not rotate, skew, stretch, or distort
- Do not swap the purple/green mapping
- Do not separate `GAM` and `ER` unless using an approved icon variant
- Do not remove the tagline in formal contexts unless using an approved simplified variant
- Do not place on cluttered backgrounds
- Maintain generous clear space around the logo

| Black | `#000000` | 0, 0, 0 | — |

### Usage rules
- Purple and green stay in their canonical roles — never invert
- Use secondary purples for supporting depth, not as primary logo replacements
- Use green for accents and highlights, not dominant surfaces
- Verify contrast for all text/background combinations
- Do not invent new brand greens or grays without approval

### Pairing guidance

| Color | Use for |
|---|---|
| `#B339C4` | Primary CTAs, headings, branded panels, social accents |
| `#84C552` | Highlights, energetic accents, icon accents, game tags |
| `#9E46AE` / `#833D90` | Depth, alternate surfaces, layered backgrounds |

## 4. Typography

### Primary typeface: AZONIX
- Geometric sans-serif
- Use for: logo, brand-critical headlines, short display text
- Never use for: long body copy

### Body text
- Clean modern sans-serif (Inter recommended for digital)
- Keep hierarchy strong and readable
- Do not stretch, condense, or substitute decorative fonts in brand-critical contexts

## 5. Visual language

### Motifs
- Scattered square / pixel-like geometric pattern
- Diagonal angular accents / stripes

### Guidance
- Use as supporting accents, never dominant
- Keep layouts clean and readable
- Use brand colors or neutral grays
- Do not overcrowd compositions

## 6. Tone and voice

### Default tone
- Energetic, modern, community-oriented
- Professional but approachable
- Gaming-literate without being exclusionary

### Language
- Default: Argentine Spanish
- English mixed in only when culturally natural for gaming contexts
- Clear, current, community-focused copy
- No forced slang

### Content principles
- Emphasize community, participation, belonging
- Reference Entre Ríos context when relevant
- Sound organized and credible, not corporate-cold
- Celebrate gaming culture without gatekeeping

## 7. Social and app icons

### Social icons
- Circular container with white symbol inside
- Primary variant: brand-colored circle
- Secondary variant: neutral gray circle
- Consistent sizing and spacing

### App icons
- Rounded-square format
- Approved backgrounds: white, black, purple
- Variants: wordmark-based, simplified geometric

## 9. Prohibited uses

- Distort the logo
- Alter logo colors outside approved variants
- Swap purple and green roles
- Use low-quality or pixelated logo assets
- Place the logo without clear space
- Use unauthorized font substitutions in brand-critical pieces
- Overload layouts with geometric accents
- Ignore contrast / accessibility
- Use unapproved purple or green values
- Mix multiple purples with no hierarchy
